The Nikon and physical Blackmagic device are not required.
All video is generated as complete 8-bit YUV 4:2:2 buffers, accompanied by 48 kHz stereo PCM packets and exact rational presentation timestamps. Each instrument enforces half-duplex; simultaneous virtual loopback uses two instruments. MIDI, analog electrical conversion, real interlaced fields, GPU/Syphon and PCI/firmware are not emulated.
