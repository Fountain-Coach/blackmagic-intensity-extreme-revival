# Sustainability ledger

**Reuse is a hypothesis to test, not a carbon-offset claim.** Repairing electronics can preserve useful materials and avoid some manufacturing and disposal impacts. But whether this specific setup reduces climate impact depends on what replacement it displaces, how long it remains useful, and how much power the legacy capture computer consumes.

## What we can currently claim

- A legacy Blackmagic Intensity Extreme and Thunderbolt dock physically exist and enumerate on a working Mac mini.
- Their original software stack is obsolete for current macOS development and capture is not yet functional.
- Research into an interoperable software interface creates a **possibility** of extended hardware use.
- We have **not** yet measured avoided greenhouse-gas emissions, energy use, years of additional service, or successful production use.

These are observations and goals, not independently verified environmental benefits.

## A transparent comparison

Compare a *defined service*, such as 100 hours of capture per year for five years, under two scenarios:

**Reuse:** keep or repair existing device + any legacy host power consumption + replacement parts + work and equipment added to enable capture + eventual recycling.

**Replacement:** manufacture and deliver a new capture device + host/adapter requirements + operational power + eventual recycling.

Useful records:

| Measurement | Unit / method | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functional capture hours delivered | Hours with reproducible video capture | Not measured |
| Power of legacy Mac mini at idle and during capture | Wall-plug watts measured by power meter | Not measured |
| Power of modern substitute setup | Wall-plug watts under comparable conditions | Not measured |
| Actual replaced device and accessories | Document models, material and manufacture estimates | Not established |
| Lifetime extended | Time in verified use after repair | Not established |
| Additional hardware purchased | Receipts and part descriptions | Not established |
| End-of-life handling | Document where equipment goes | Not established |

For an illustrative *operational energy* difference only:

`additional energy (kWh) = [legacy setup watts - alternative setup watts] × hours / 1000`

That number is not a carbon footprint. Material production, transport, replacement, disposal, and regional power mix must also be considered before any net greenhouse-gas estimate.

## Responsible communication

Preferred: **“Investigating how software repair may extend the service life of usable video hardware and help reduce unnecessary electronic waste.”**

Avoid unsubstantiated claims such as “zero-carbon capture”, “carbon-neutral”, or a stated number of kilograms of carbon dioxide saved before reproducible evidence exists.

Our ambition is to make repairability tangible and count the benefits honestly, including cases where a newer device would be more energy efficient.
